    PRIVACY, EXPLAINED

    Know where your
    example goes.

    Your browser sends your example to this application server for AI analysis. The application does not store password entries or include them in its logs.

    For the optional breach check, the server creates a hash and sends only its first five characters to Have I Been Pwned. Neither the password nor its complete hash is sent to that service.

    Use invented examples. A low risk score or a password absent from breach data does not guarantee safety. Public deployment requires HTTPS.
